# Personalization checklist

Complete this before sharing the portfolio with employers.

- [ ] Replace `Your Name` throughout the site.
- [ ] Replace `your.email@example.com` with a professional email address.
- [ ] Add your LinkedIn and GitHub profile URLs.
- [ ] Write an accurate professional summary on the About page.
- [ ] Add 2–3 verifiable experience highlights or outcomes.
- [ ] Replace the tools placeholders with tools you have actually used.
- [ ] Review the fictional Atlas examples and retain their sample disclosures, or replace them with approved work.
- [ ] Remove any claims, metrics, or technologies that do not reflect your experience.
- [ ] Proofread and test every page and link.
- [ ] Ask a peer to review the portfolio before sending it to recruiters.

## Good evidence to add

Where confidentiality permits, strengthen each sample with a short case-study note: audience, problem, your role, constraints, process, and outcome. Avoid confidential information and do not invent metrics.
