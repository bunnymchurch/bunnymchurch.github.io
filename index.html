Documentation principles

  • Task-oriented: organize content around what readers need to accomplish.
  • Consistent: use predictable terminology, structure, and formatting.
  • Actionable: provide prerequisites, steps, examples, and expected results.
  • Accessible: write in plain language and make information easy to scan.
