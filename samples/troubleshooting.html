SAMPLE 03 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Troubleshoot workspace sync issues

Use this guide when changes in Atlas do not appear across your devices.

Audience: End usersContent type: Troubleshooting workflowProduct: Fictional sample
Sample note: Atlas is a fictional product. The symptoms and resolutions below demonstrate a support-documentation pattern.

Symptoms

You may have a sync issue if:

  • A change appears on one device but not another.
  • The sync indicator continues to show that syncing is in progress.
  • You see a message that Atlas could not sync your changes.

Before you begin

  • Confirm that your device has an active internet connection.
  • Make sure you are signed in to the same Atlas account on each device.
  • Keep any unsaved work open until you confirm that it has synced.

Resolve the issue

1. Check your connection

  1. Open another website or app that requires internet access.
  2. If it does not load, reconnect to a working network.
  3. Return to Atlas and check the sync indicator.

If syncing resumes: No further action is needed. If the issue continues, go to step 2.

2. Confirm your account

  1. Open your account menu in Atlas.
  2. Check the signed-in email address.
  3. If it is not the account you use on your other device, sign out and sign in with the correct account.

If the accounts match: Continue to step 3.

3. Refresh the workspace

  1. Wait a few moments to allow any pending changes to sync.
  2. Refresh the Atlas page or close and reopen the app.
  3. Check whether the missing changes appear.

If the issue continues

Contact your support team and include the following information:

  • The approximate time the issue started, including your time zone.
  • The affected workspace and device type.
  • The exact error message, if one appears.
  • The troubleshooting steps you have already tried.
Important: Do not send passwords, API tokens, or other credentials in a support request.

Writing decisions demonstrated

  • Symptoms help readers confirm that the article matches their issue.
  • Steps are ordered from simple checks to more involved actions.
  • Conditional guidance tells readers what to do next.
  • Escalation details help support teams investigate efficiently.
